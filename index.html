LONG RANGE SABER DUELS

Two duelists on the moon, seven and a half metres apart. Your sabers are glass, and the glowing liquid inside them is your ammunition.

  1. Hold the trigger — the blade winds up and the liquid churns bright.
  2. Swing — the blade records the surface it carves through the air.
  3. Release — that shape is thrown, flying the way you cut, at the speed you cut.
  4. Shake the saber with the trigger released to refill it. Keep a rhythm and it fills faster.
  5. Duck and lean to dodge. There is no dodge button — you move your body.
